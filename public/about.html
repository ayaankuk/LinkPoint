About

We find the counter-tariffs importers paid but didn't owe.

Canada's counter-tariffs apply only to goods made in the U.S. But invoices often list where goods shipped from, not where they were made, and tariff codes get misclassified. Tariff Trace AI reads every line, traces the real origin, and hands a licensed customs broker a claim that's ready to file.

$10.3B

collected by Canada in customs and import duties in 2025. A national total, not the amount overpaid.

1 in 3

businesses with 1–19 employees said Canadian tariffs on U.S. goods they bought hurt their business.

Nearly 1 in 2

businesses with 20–99 employees said the same.

Sources: Statistics Canada, customs and import duties, 2025; Canadian Survey on Business Conditions, Q2 2026.

What we do

Read invoices and customs entries, trace origin from supplier pages, flag wrong codes and drawback, then prepare the claim and track it until the refund lands.

What brokers do

A licensed customs broker reviews the evidence, signs off and files with CBSA. We are not a customs broker and never file on our own.

How we're paid

Importers pay a setup fee and 20% of refunds when they're paid. Brokers pay per prepared claim. We earn when claims are worth filing.

Built on Cloudflare

Documents, AI and claim data all run inside one Cloudflare account. Invoices are never sent to an outside AI provider.
