Why the money goes unclaimed

Invoices show where goods shipped from

Counter-tariffs apply to goods made in the U.S. A U.S. warehouse address on the invoice doesn't make the goods American.

Only the importer of record can claim

Suppliers won't file for you. The claim has to come from your business, with evidence attached.

The paperwork costs more than the claim

Chasing origin documents line by line takes hours. We do that part, so a broker only has to review and file.

Every line item is checked against four recovery and savings paths.

public

Goods shipped from a U.S. warehouse but made in Korea, Mexico, or Europe should not carry U.S. counter-tariffs.

Export drawback

If imported inputs go into goods you export back out of Canada, you can claim back the duty and surtax paid.

Common questions

What does the free scan include? expand_more

We read every line of your commercial invoice or customs entry and show how much is recoverable now, what needs an origin document, and what you can avoid on your next order.

Is Tariff Trace AI a customs broker? expand_more

No. Tariff Trace AI prepares your claim package and origin evidence, and a licensed Canadian customs broker reviews and files it with CBSA.

What if my invoice does not list the country of origin? expand_more

Our system checks your supplier's product pages for country-of-origin declarations and captures timestamped evidence for the broker.

How long does a CBSA refund take? expand_more

Scanning takes minutes, broker filing typically happens within 1 week, and CBSA refunds usually land in about 3 months.

Where are my uploaded invoices stored? expand_more

In private storage on Cloudflare R2. Files are only shared with the licensed broker filing your claim, and the AI that reads them runs on the same network.
